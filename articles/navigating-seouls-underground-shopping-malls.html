Navigating Seoul's Underground Shopping Malls

GoSeoul Edit Team • Updated Mar 2026 • 4 min read

Seoul possesses an incredible superpower: an entire secondary city exists underground. Driven by extreme weather (freezing winters and monsoon summers), the city has developed massive subterranean shopping complexes connected directly to major subway stations. You can spend an entire day shopping, dining, and navigating the city without once seeing the sky.

1. Goto Mall (Express Bus Terminal Station)

Located at the intersection of Lines 3, 7, and 9, Goto Mall is the undisputed king of underground shopping in Seoul. It stretches for nearly a kilometer underground, containing over 600 stores.

What to expect: This is ground zero for fast fashion, shoes, bags, and surprisingly, home decor and artificial flowers. The clothing here is incredibly cheap (often 10,000 to 20,000 KRW per item) but heavily leans towards women's free-size garments. It is loud, crowded, and relentless. Ensure you bring cash, as vendors will heavily discount items for cash transactions over credit cards.

2. Gangnam Station Underground Shopping Center

Gangnam Station is one of the busiest subway stations on Earth, and the area outside the turnstiles is a massive shopping arena. Unlike Goto Mall's endless hallway, Gangnam is more of a sprawling labyrinth.

What to expect: Because it sits at the heart of the business and K-Beauty district, the underground mall here is slightly more polished. It is the absolute best place to find inexpensive but high-quality cosmetics, hundreds of distinct smartphone case boutiques, and trendy streetwear aimed at university students.

3. Bupyeong Underground Market (Incheon)

If the 600 stores at Goto Mall weren't enough, take the subway to Bupyeong Station in neighboring Incheon. With over 1,400 stores, it holds the Guinness World Record for the largest underground shopping center in the world.

What to expect: It is so large and complicated that the local government essentially instituted a color-coded pathway system so people wouldn't get perpetually lost. You can find absolutely everything here, usually cheaper than in the center of Seoul.

Navigational Tips for the Underground

It is incredibly easy to lose your bearings underground, as GPS signals fail. Always memorize the specific Exit Number (e.g., "Exit 8") you came from or are heading towards. The yellow directional signs hanging from the ceiling are your only true guidepoints in the subterranean maze. Trust the yellow signs.
