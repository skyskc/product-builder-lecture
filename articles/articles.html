Seoul Travel Guides

Welcome to the ultimate library of Seoul travel guides. Planning a trip to South Korea's massive, sprawling capital can feel overwhelming. With 25 distinct districts (gu), a transit system that spans hundreds of miles, and a culture that seamlessly blends ancient neon-lit traditions with hyper-modern technology, knowing where to start is half the battle.

Our editorial team at GoSeoul has spent years navigating the city to bring you these comprehensive, deeply researched articles. We don't just list tourist traps; we break down the nuances of Korean culture so you can travel like a seasoned local. Whether you are trying to mathematically deduce if the Discover Seoul Pass is worth your money, seeking the courage to strip down in a traditional public bathhouse (Jjimjilbang), or simply looking for the safest, most delicious street food in Myeongdong, we have you covered.

Why read our guides? Unlike simple aggregators, every piece of content here is designed to provide actionable value. We cover the high-energy nightlife of Gangnam and Hongdae, the quiet Zen of Insadong's traditional teahouses, and the rugged, breathtaking hiking trails of Bukhansan National Park. Furthermore, we provide essential logistical advice—from acquiring and charging your Tmoney transit card to navigating the massive, labyrinthian underground shopping malls when the monsoon rains hit.

Browse our collection below, ranging from perfect 3-day itineraries for first-timers to highly specialized deep-dives into K-Beauty clinics and K-Pop experiences. If you find a spot you love in our articles, remember to jump over to our Route Planner to seamlessly insert it into your daily schedule.
